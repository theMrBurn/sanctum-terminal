# Reference (the answer key)

Links you may consult **only after** taking your own swing at each
module. Treat as solutions to a chess puzzle — useful for verifying,
poisonous if read first.

---

## Mark Moxon — bbcelite.com

Exhaustive annotated disassembly of every BBC Elite variant (Cassette,
Disc, 6502SP, NES, Apple II) plus Master and Electron.

- <https://bbcelite.com/>
- MIT-licensed annotations
- Walks every routine with prose explanation
- Cross-references between variants

The single most complete RE work on Elite ever produced. Your
reference of last resort.

---

## Ian Bell — Elite home page

Original Elite co-author's site. Hosts the released disk images,
historical context, and his own original notes.

- <http://www.iancgbell.clara.net/elite/>

---

## Christian Pinder — Elite: The New Kind (1999)

C port of BBC Elite for Windows / Linux. Released with source.
Reads like a near-line-by-line C translation of the 6502 — useful
for confirming algorithm shapes once you've worked them out.

- Source available via various mirrors; search "Elite The New Kind
  source"

---

## Oolite

Modern open-source Elite-faithful remake (3D, OpenGL, much bigger
than what we're working with). Not directly useful for RE but the
design docs at <https://www.oolite.space/> describe Braben's
algorithms in approachable prose.

---

## JS Elite

Elite '84 in ~53 KB of JavaScript, runs in a browser, all 8 galaxies
playable.

- <https://github.com/fesh0r/newkind> or similar JS-port repos
- Reads as another reference implementation

---

## Acornsoft Elite manuals

The original 1984 player manual is widely scanned and online. Useful
context for "what is the player expected to know" — informs the
heuristics the AI uses.

---

## Discipline

Open these *after* you've taken your own swing at the module's
problem. Read them as **verification**, not as **learning material**.

A passing test against your own implementation that diverges from
Moxon is worth more than a test that copies Moxon. Find your version
of the truth first.
